# The labelled set

Empty, and it is the most important thing missing.

This directory will hold a few hundred items, drawn from real benchmarks and labelled by hand, together with the command that scores the matchers against them. Until it exists, `editscreen` reports what it found and cannot say how often it is wrong.

The labelling has to come first for a second reason. The three levels in `types.py` were written from a handful of examples. Labelling real items is what will show whether they are the right three — and the expectation is that two or three items will fit none of them.
